## Interpretation

The expanded campaign changes the conclusion from the uniform-ISRF post-processing: spatially varying illumination can readily generate statistically significant spatial variation in the synthetic M--T relation. The best F-statistics exceed the IC5146 target value (F=6.78), but the strongest F cases do not fully reproduce the observed IC5146 slope-range amplitude (13.5 K/dex) and generally lack inversion. The inversion cases are therefore especially important targets for follow-up inspection, even when their F-statistics are not the global maximum.

The clean separation between uniform controls and gradient models supports the interpretation that the missing variable in v1 was external radiation-field spatial structure rather than column-density shielding alone.

Caveats: the runner fixed package-level issues in the submitted scripts (Athena++ reader import, negative-axis optical-depth accumulation, and 3D temperature indexing). The analysis uses the same simplified least-shielded-direction attenuation approximation as the bundle, so these results should be treated as a diagnostic post-processing experiment rather than full radiative transfer.
