PHYSICAL INTERPRETATION
  1. Magnetic support (lower β) extends the longitudinal beading
     regime to higher f, consistent with magnetic tension stabilising
     against radial collapse.
  2. Supersonic motions (M = 2) compress the filament, triggering
     earlier radial collapse and lowering f_transition.
  3. The transition is predominantly abrupt (Δf < 0.2), indicating
     a sharp boundary between beading and radial collapse regimes.
  4. λ/W decreases with increasing f through the transition zone,
     approaching ~2–3 before beading ceases.

IMPLICATIONS FOR HGBS
  HGBS filaments with f ≈ 1.5–3.0 reside at or beyond f_transition.
  This confirms the referee's concern about regime mismatch.
  Combined with turbulent support (Campaign TURB), f_eff places
  these filaments back into the near-critical beading regime.
