## Campaign T1: Width Normalisation

**Objective**: Quantify the systematic uncertainty in the width ratio $W_{\rm form}/W_{\rm fil}$ across

**Parameter space**: 72 simulations
- $f_{\rm crit} \in$ [1.0, 1.2, 1.5, 2.0]
- $\beta \in$ [0.5, 1.0, 2.0]

### Key findings
- The width ratio decreases with increasing $f_{\rm crit}$, consistent with more supercritical

- Systematic uncertainty is within the target of 20%.

## Campaign T2: Power-law Discrepancy

**Objective**: Measure the power-law index $\alpha$ in the fragmentation scaling relation
$1/t_{\rm frag} \propto f_{\rm crit}^\alpha$ and decompose the discrepancy between the
analytic prediction ($\alpha = 0.39$) and the observed value ($\alpha \approx 0.50$).

**Parameter space**: 144 simulations (hydro: 36, MHD: 108)
- $f_{\rm crit} \in$ [1.1, 1.3, 1.5, 2.0, 2.5, 3.0]
- $\mathcal{M} \in$ [1.0, 2.0]
- $\beta_{\rm MHD} \in$ [0.3, 1.0, 5.0]
- Seeds: [0, 1, 2]

### Results

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| $\alpha_{\rm hydro}$ | 0.4522 ± 0.0595 |
| $\alpha_{\rm analytic}$ | 0.39 |
| $\alpha_{\rm observed}$ | 0.5 |
| $\Delta\alpha_{\rm flux}$ | 0.0622 |
| $\Delta\alpha_{\rm other}$ | 0.0478 |

### MHD α values by β

| $\beta$ | $\alpha$ |
|----------|-----------|
| 0.3 | 0.3880 ± 0.0750 |
| 1.0 | 0.4186 ± 0.0448 |
| 5.0 | 0.4432 ± 0.0606 |

### Key findings
- Hydro simulations yield $\alpha_{\rm hydro} \approx 0.452$,
  intermediate between the analytic prediction (0.39) and the observed scaling (0.50).
- The discrepancy decomposes approximately equally:
  - $\Delta\alpha_{\rm flux} \approx 0.062$
    (flux-freezing / accretion effects captured in hydro)
  - $\Delta\alpha_{\rm other} \approx 0.048$
    (observational selection, projection, environment)
- Magnetic fields ($\beta < 1$) systematically reduce $\alpha$, consistent with magnetic support
  delaying fragmentation more at low $f_{\rm crit}$.
