## XII. Success Criteria: Genuine Novelty

### How Do We Know It's Working?

**Indicators of genuine theoretical innovation:**

1. **Mathematical novelty**: New equations, structures, operators
2. **Conceptual novelty**: New concepts, frameworks, paradigms
3. **Unification**: Connecting previously disconnected phenomena
4. **Surprising predictions**: Testable but unexpected
5. **Paradox resolution**: Solving longstanding problems
6. **New questions**: Generating new research directions
7. **Community response**: Cited, discussed, tested, debated

**Red flags (pseudo-innovation):**
- Just reparameterizing existing theories
- Numerology without justification
- Mathematical curiosities without physical meaning
- Unfalsifiable "theories"
- Purely phenomenological fits
