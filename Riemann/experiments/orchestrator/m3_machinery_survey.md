## 2. Top-three work orders (synthesis, verbatim substance)

**WO-1 Connes NCG (0.50) — instrumentation + convergence-proxy measurement, not a proof bet.**
First lemma is NOT semilocal-to-global convergence (a limit statement finite computation cannot
decide) but its finite-stage prerequisites: (i) uniform-in-S self-adjointness/spectral-convergence
control of the finite-stage operators, measurable as stability of extremal spectra under S-growth;
(ii) a p-adic analogue of the archimedean prolate/Sonin/Toeplitz instrument at even one finite
place — no analogue exists for any p; a p=2 toy theorem would be the family's first finite-place
positivity. First finite computation: reproduce end-to-end the S = {primes < 13} extremization
(claimed in arXiv:2602.04022 `[UNVERIFIED]`: first 50 zeros matched to 2.6e-55), then extend S and
measure spectral stability with certified error bounds. **Mandatory adjunct: the
structural-automaticity test** — run the identical extremization on a modified finite Euler product
containing an off-line zero BY CONSTRUCTION; if finite-stage spectra still sit on Re = 1/2, the
on-the-line theorem is forced by self-adjointness regardless of truth and the finite agreement
transfers no mechanism to the limit. (This adjunct is the reward-hacking audit of the 2026 claim,
in the Letter-30 sense: does the instrument measure the thing or its own construction?)

**WO-2 Transfer operators (0.40) — bounded probe with pre-registered kill criteria.**
Eisenstein-sector pinning lemma: an anti-linear involution J with Q_q* = J Q_{1−q} J^{-1}
(reversibility/PT-type symmetry) forcing eigenvalue-1 points of the Farey/Mayer family onto
Re q = 1/4. The swarm's arXiv full-text sweep verifies this question has NEVER been posed
(arXiv "Riemann hypothesis"+"transfer operator" = 2 hits, both Hilgert–Mayer spin models)
`[UNVERIFIED — spot-check before relying]`. Guides: Hilgert–Mayer Kac–Baker zeros on Re s = ln 2
(in-family existence proof), Pohl's infinite-area resonances filling a half-plane (in-family
negative control), obstruction to beat: Prellberg's continuous spectrum [0,1] for q ≥ 1
(zeta-carrying eigenfunctions non-normalizable). First finite computation: truncated Q_{q,z}
rigorously computable via Bonanno's infinite-matrix formulation — compute the eigenvalue-1 variety
at increasing truncation; measure (a) J-symmetry defect for candidate involutions, (b) whether
Eisenstein-branch eigenvalue-1 points drift toward Re q = 1/4 or scatter. No drift + no surviving
J-candidate ⇒ close the lane cheaply.

**WO-3 Nyman–Beurling (0.30) — attack the one named open target.**
