# Paper Restructuring Plan

## Current Problems

1. **Hodge-podge structure** - Built up from iterative referee responses
2. **Defensive "Executive Summary of Limitations"** - Unusual and defensive
3. **Excessive sub-subsections** - Especially in referee response campaigns
4. **Inconsistent narrative** - Old "discrepancy" vs new "geometric diversity" framing
5. **Abstract too long** - Multiple paragraphs, should be 200-300 words
6. **Repetitive content** - Same results mentioned multiple times

## New Streamlined Structure

### 1. Abstract (200-300 words)
- Core result: HGBS λ/W = 2.84 ± 0.12
- Main finding: Field geometry is primary driver (2.75× effect: 1.25 vs 3.4)
- Validation: Regional variation (1.98-3.46) spans theoretical range
- Conclusion: Observations reflect physical diversity, not discrepancy with theory

### 2. Introduction (shorter)
- Classical theory context (λ/W ≈ 4 for idealized cylinders)
- Observational context (HGBS measurements)
- Key question: Why the variation across regions?
- Preview: Magnetic field geometry explains the diversity

### 3. Observational Analysis (condensed)
- HGBS sample and methodology (keep concise)
- Results: Table 1 with regional measurements
- Note: Regional scatter is PHYSICAL, not noise

### 4. Theoretical Framework (short)
- Classical theory assumptions
- Magnetic field geometry expectations
- λ/W predictions: perpendicular (1.25), longitudinal (3.4)

### 5. MHD Simulations (reorganized by physics, not campaigns)
**5.1 Simulation Methodology** (standard)
**5.2 Field Geometry Results** (merged Campaigns 5, 6, 7)
- Near-critical fragmentation (Phase 1)
- Perpendicular vs longitudinal comparison
- β-dependence results

**5.3 Three-Regime Framework** (keep)
**5.4 Supercritical Campaign** (condensed)

### 6. Geometric Mixture Validation (NEW FOCUS)
- Regional variation spans theoretical range
- All observations lie within [1.25, 3.40] bounds
- Mean lies between extremes as expected from mixture
- This IS the validation

### 7. Discussion (reframed)
- Remove "Can Models Explain the Observational Discrepancy?" title
- Focus on interpretation: Why regions differ
- Address limitations concisely
- Future work: Polarimetry to test predictions

### 8. Conclusions (5 bullet points, not 15+)
- Field geometry as primary driver
- Regional variation as validation
- No universal λ/W exists
- Testable predictions for future work
- Implications for star formation

4. **Multiple validation subsections** → Consolidate

## Consistency Checks

1. **Framing**: Throughout, replace "discrepancy with theory" with "diversity as expected from geometry"
2. **λ/W values**: Ensure consistent use of 1.25 (perpendicular) and 3.4 (longitudinal)
3. **Regional interpretation**: consistently interpret low λ/W as perpendicular, high λ/W as longitudinal
4. **Remove**: All references to "explaining the discrepancy" or "why observations differ from theory"

## Key Narrative Change

**OLD**: "Why do observations differ from classical theory? Here are our attempts to explain it."

**NEW**: "Observations show the expected diversity from magnetic field geometry. Here is the validation."
