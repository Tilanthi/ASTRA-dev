# Critical Issues with Nearest-Neighbor Analysis

**Date**: 2026-05-02

---

## Problem Summary

The paper's nearest-neighbor (NN) analysis has serious methodological flaws that undermine the central claim about the L/3 convergence artifact.

---

## Issue 1: The "Corrected" NN Values Are Not Actual Measurements

The file `corrected_nn_spacing_results.json` was generated by `compute_corrected_nn_spacing.py`, which applies a 7.0× "bias correction factor" to pairwise median values:

```python
PAIRWISE_BIAS_FACTOR = 7.0  # Line 74
nn_corrected = pairwise / PAIRWISE_BIAS_FACTOR  # Line 104
```

**Example for Taurus**:
- Pairwise median: 0.198 pc
- "Corrected" NN: 0.198 / 7.0 = 0.0283 pc

This is not a measurement from HGBS catalogues. It is a **mathematical transformation** of the pairwise median value.

---

## Issue 2: Raw NN Measurements Contradict the L/3 Artifact Claim

The file `full_nn_results_COMPLETE.json` contains **actual NN measurements** from HGBS skeleton and core catalogues:

| Region | N | NN (pc) | PM (pc) | NN/PM Ratio | λ/W (NN) | λ/W (PM) |
|--------|---|---------|---------|-------------|----------|----------|
| Taurus | 536 | 0.062 | 0.198 | 3.18 (NN smaller!) | 0.62 | 1.98 |
| Perseus | 816 | 0.182 | 0.248 | 1.37 (NN smaller!) | 1.82 | 2.48 |
| Aquila | 710 | 0.161 | 0.346 | 2.15 (NN smaller!) | 1.61 | 3.46 |
| Orion B | - | *failed* | 0.313 | - | - | 3.13 |

**Critical finding**: NN < PM for all regions where measurements exist. This is the **OPPOSITE** of what the L/3 convergence artifact would predict.

If PM truly converged to L/3 and L/3 < λ (true spacing), then we would observe **PM < NN**. Instead, we observe **NN < PM**.

---

## Issue 3: No Analytical or Numerical Demonstration of L/3 Artifact

The paper claims:
> "the pairwise median approaches min(λ, L/3) as N → ∞"

This requires:
1. Analytical derivation for a perfectly periodic distribution
2. Numerical demonstration with synthetic data of known λ
3. Published peer-reviewed validation

**None of these are provided**. The only reference is to "Campaign 10 bias correction (April 2026)," which is not a citable publication.

---

## Issue 4: Internal Inconsistency in the Paper

The paper presents contradictory information:

1. **Abstract**: Claims NN result of λ/W = 4.15 is primary
2. **Table 2**: Shows NN values derived from PM × 1.43 (not actual measurements)
3. **Tables 1, 3, Figure 1**: Still use PM values throughout
4. **Discussion Section**: Still explains why λ/W = 2.79 differs from theory—a question supposedly resolved by the NN result

---

## What the Raw Data Actually Shows

### Raw NN Measurements (from HGBS catalogues)

**Robust regions with successful NN analysis**:
- Perseus: NN = 0.182 ± 0.010 pc, λ/W = 1.82
- **Taurus**: NN = 0.062 ± 0.005 pc, λ/W = 0.62
- Aquila: NN = 0.161 ± 0.013 pc, λ/W = 1.61

**All regions are sub-Jeans** (λ/W < 4), even more so than the PM measurements.

### Statistical Comparison

| Metric | PM Result | Raw NN Result | Interpretation |
|--------|-----------|---------------|----------------|
| Weighted mean (robust) | 0.279 pc (2.79×) | ~0.12 pc (1.2×, estimated) | NN is MORE sub-Jeans |
| Taurus | 1.98× | 0.62× | NN is 3.2× smaller |
| Perseus | 2.48× | 1.82× | NN is 1.4× smaller |

The raw NN measurements **confirm** the sub-Jeans spacing and even strengthen the case.

## Recommended Path Forward

### Option A: Retract the L/3 Artifact Claim

1. Acknowledge that the L/3 convergence artifact is **not demonstrated**
2. Remove all references to "7.0× bias correction"
3. Report raw NN measurements where available (Perseus, Aquila)
4. Note that NN analysis confirms sub-Jeans spacing (λ/W ≈ 1.2-1.8 for robust regions)

### Option B: Provide Rigorous Validation

1. **Analytical derivation**: For N points with spacing λ on length L = Nλ, compute the median of all N(N-1)/2 pairwise distances
2. **Numerical demonstration**: Generate synthetic periodic distributions with known λ, compute PM and NN, show convergence
3. **Apply to HGBS**: Re-run NN analysis on all robust regions (Orion B failed in original analysis)

### Option C: Revert to Pairwise Median as Primary

1. Acknowledge limitations of PM statistic
2. Note that limited NN data exists and is consistent with PM trend
3. Focus on explaining sub-Jeans spacing rather than claiming measurement artifact

---

## Critical Questions for the Authors

1. **Where is the Campaign 10 documentation?** The simulations, setup, and analysis that produced the 7.0× factor need to be fully described.

2. **Why does raw NN < PM?** This is opposite to the L/3 artifact prediction. If PM converges to L/3 and L/3 < λ, then PM should be SMALLER than NN.

3. **Why are the NN/PM ratios suspiciously constant?** Table 2 shows 1.43 for Taurus, Perseus, Orion B despite very different N values (536, 816, 1844). If the artifact scales with N, the ratios should vary.

4. **Can we get NN measurements for all robust regions?** Orion B analysis failed. Without it, we cannot make robust regional comparisons.

---

## Conclusion

The paper's central claim—that the L/3 convergence artifact explains a 35-50% PM underestimate—is **not supported** by:
1. Actual NN measurements from HGBS catalogues (which show NN < PM)
2. Analytical derivation of L/3 convergence
3. Peer-reviewed validation of the 7.0× correction factor

The raw NN data, where available, **confirms** the sub-Jeans spacing and does NOT resolve the discrepancy with classical theory.

**Recommendation**: Retract the L/3 artifact claim and revert to the original pairwise median analysis with appropriate acknowledgment of its limitations, OR provide rigorous analytical/numerical validation before making such a claim.
