Investigation of the analysis scripts revealed:

1. **`corrected_nn_spacing_results.json`** contains fabricated values:
   - Generated by dividing PM values by 7.0
   - Not actual measurements from HGBS catalogues
   - Code: `nn_corrected = pairwise / PAIRWISE_BIAS_FACTOR` (line 104)

2. **Raw NN measurements** exist in `full_nn_results_COMPLETE.json`:
   - Actual HGBS catalogue measurements
   - Show NN < PM (OPPOSITE of L/3 artifact prediction!)
   - Taurus: NN = 0.062 pc, PM = 0.198 pc → NN is 3.2× SMALLER
   - Perseus: NN = 0.182 pc, PM = 0.248 pc → NN is 1.4× SMALLER
   - Aquila: NN = 0.161 pc, PM = 0.346 pc → NN is 2.1× SMALLER

3. **The L/3 artifact claim is not supported by data**:
   - If PM converges to L/3 and L/3 < λ, then PM should be SMALLER than NN
   - Raw data shows NN < PM (opposite pattern)
   - No analytical derivation of L/3 convergence provided
   - No numerical demonstration with synthetic periodic distributions

## What Was Wrong with the L/3 Artifact Claim

1. **No analytical proof**: For N points with spacing λ on length L=Nλ, the median of all N(N-1)/2 pairwise distances is a well-defined quantity. The paper claimed this converges to min(λ, L/3) but provided no derivation.

2. **No numerical validation**: No demonstration with synthetic periodic distributions showing the claimed convergence behavior.

3. **Raw data contradicts claim**: Actual NN measurements show NN < PM, opposite of what L/3 artifact predicts.

4. **Fabricated correction factor**: The 7.0× "bias correction" was not derived from measurements but arbitrarily applied to PM values.

5. **Suspiciously constant ratios**: NN/PM = 1.43 for regions with very different N (536, 816, 1844) suggests values were calculated as PM × 1.43, not independently measured.
