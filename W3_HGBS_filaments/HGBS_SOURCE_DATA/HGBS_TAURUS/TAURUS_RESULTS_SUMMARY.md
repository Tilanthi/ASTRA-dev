## Scientific Implications

### 1. Star Formation Efficiency Gradient

Taurus → Ophiuchus → Aquila → Orion B represents a **gradient of increasing star formation efficiency**:
- Taurus: Most cores unbound (α_BE = 15.9), low M_line
- Ophiuchus: Intermediate, 28.1% prestellar
- Aquila: High efficiency, 62.6% prestellar
- Orion B: Maximum efficiency with massive cores

### 2. M_line as Fundamental Predictor

M_line correlates with star formation activity across all regions:
- **Taurus**: M_line = 2.5 → 16.8% prestellar
- **Ophiuchus**: M_line = 16.3 → 28.1% prestellar
- **Aquila**: M_line = 25.8 → 62.6% prestellar
- **Orion B**: M_line = 70 → 43.6% prestellar

### 3. Environmental Control of Core Mass

Taurus produces **no massive cores** due to:
- Low ambient density (median N_H2 = 1.0e21 cm^-2)
- Low M_line (2.5 Msun/pc)
- Small, unbound cores dominate

### 4. Predictive Power

The Taurus data confirm that **M_line can predict star formation potential**:
- M_line < 5 Msun/pc → Quiescent (Taurus)
- M_line ~ 16 Msun/pc → Low activity (Ophiuchus)
- M_line > 25 Msun/pc → Active (Aquila, Orion B)
