## Genuine Discovery Criteria Framework

### Level 1: Novel Phenomenon Discovery ⭐⭐⭐

**Definition**: First observation of previously unknown astronomical phenomenon

**Required Elements**:
- Previously unobserved physical phenomenon
- Quantitative characterization with measurements
- Reproducible detection methodology
- Statistical significance (p<0.001 or equivalent)
- Peer-review ready documentation

**Examples**:
- First detection of gravitational waves from neutron star mergers
- Discovery of fast radio bursts (FRBs) as astronomical phenomenon
- Identification of tabby's star light patterns

**EUREKA Score Required**: >0.90 with NEW_PHENOMENON claim type

### Level 2: New Mechanism Discovery ⭐⭐⭐

**Definition**: Discovery of previously unknown physical mechanism or explanation

**Required Elements**:
- Novel explanatory mechanism
- Mathematical/theoretical framework
- Predictive capability
- Testable predictions confirmed
- Contradicts or extends established understanding

**Examples**:
- Dark energy as cosmological constant vs. quintessence
- New star formation mechanisms in extreme environments
- Alternative gravitational theories explaining anomalies

**EUREKA Score Required**: >0.85 with NEW_MECHANISM claim type

### Level 3: Methodological Innovation ⭐⭐

**Definition**: Significant improvement in detection/analysis methods enabling new discoveries

**Required Elements**:
- 10x+ improvement in existing metrics
- Enables previously impossible observations
- Theoretical validation of improvement
- Adoptable by broader community
- Demonstrated on real data

**Examples**:
- Breakthrough in RV precision (cm/s → mm/s)
- Novel transit analysis revealing previously undetectable planets
- New statistical methods for period detection

**EUREKA Score Required**: >0.80 with NEW_METHOD claim type

### Level 4: Unexpected Relationships/Correlations ⭐⭐

**Definition**: Discovery of previously unrecognized relationships between known phenomena

**Required Elements**:
- Novel correlation between established phenomena
- Statistical significance beyond random chance
- Theoretical explanation for relationship
- Predictive capability
- Reproducible across multiple datasets

**Examples**:
- Correlation between star formation rates and galactic environments
- Relationship between exoplanet types and host star properties
- Unexpected patterns in CMB anisotropies

**EUREKA Score Required**: >0.75 with NEW_RELATIONSHIP claim type

## Quantitative Thresholds for Genuine Discovery

### Literature Similarity Requirements

**Genuine Discovery Ranges**:
- **Novel Phenomenon**: <70% similarity (fundamentally new)
- **New Mechanism**: <75% similarity (significant conceptual advance)
- **Method Innovation**: <80% similarity (practical advance)
- **New Relationship**: <85% similarity (correlational discovery)

**Rejection Ranges** (current candidates):
- **Established Knowledge**: >90% similarity (automatic rejection)
- **Well-Documented**: 85-90% similarity (strong rejection)
- **Similar Concepts Exist**: 80-85% similarity (moderate rejection)

**Genuine Discovery Thresholds**:
- **Claim Novelty**: >0.70 (specific claims not found in literature)

- **Eureka Score**: >0.85 (overall genuine advance assessment)
- **Claim Confidence**: >0.75 (high confidence in claim validity)
